Should we send this shopper a discount—or would they buy anyway?

1. Define the decision before predicting it

Start with the first observed cart for each shopper–product pair. Wait 24 hours; exclude pairs already purchased or currently removed. Observe purchases for seven more days. Require 30 days of warm-up and full follow-up. Repeated journeys for the same pair are excluded.

The dataset records June–December 2022 activity. “RecSys 2025” is the challenge release label. Of 225,224,262 source events, this model uses cart and purchase histories; cart removals establish eligibility. Page visits and searches are inventoried but excluded from predictors.

2. Evaluate on later shoppers’ decisions

The selected model is gradient boosting, chosen on validation average precision. Training outcomes end before validation begins, and validation outcomes end before test decisions begin. Shopper IDs, product IDs and future behavior are excluded from predictors.

3. Explore the economics instead of assuming the answer

Target the lowest purchase-propensity 20%, compare with random targeting at the same contact count, blanket offers, and no new offers. Lower purchase propensity does not prove a shopper is persuadable. Adjust the selective response multiplier to test that risk.

Formula: incremental contribution = − existing buyers contacted × value × discount + nonbuyers contacted × assumed response × value × (margin − discount) − contact cost. This is a single-unit scenario using historical outcomes as a proxy for no-new-offer behavior. Actual quantities, costs, returns and historical coupons are unavailable.

4. Test the recommendation in a real experiment

Randomize consenting shoppers within propensity bands to a coupon or no new offer. Use customer-level assignment, actual contribution net of discounts and returns, and a prespecified seven-day outcome. Measure incremental profit and monitor opt-outs. Determine sample size from a pilot before launching. The historical dataset cannot establish that sending or withholding a coupon causes any improvement.
